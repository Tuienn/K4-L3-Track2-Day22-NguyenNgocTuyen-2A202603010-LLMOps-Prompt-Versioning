Additionally, dense retrieval captures semantic similarity, allowing it to match queries with relevant documents even when they share no common keywords.

A high score indicates that the answer is focused and directly responsive to the supported by retrieved context.

It compares each sentence of the reference answer against the retrieved context to check whether the context contains the information needed to derive that sentence. A high recall score means the retriever successfully fetched the information needed.
